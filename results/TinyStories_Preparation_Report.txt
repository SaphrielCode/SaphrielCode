Selection:
- Uniform row sampling without replacement across the entire supplied train split: 20,000 requested rows, Python Random.sample seed 1729.
- Independently sample 2,000 rows from the supplied validation split using seed 1730. The first 1,000 sampled indices belong to validation and the next 1,000 to final test.
- Store selected records in source-row order; the later training batch order remains controlled separately by seed 43.
- Normalize NFC Unicode, CRLF/CR to LF, and outer whitespace only. Preserve internal whitespace and case.
- Remove exact normalized duplicates globally, keeping test first, then validation, then training. Do not refill removed rows after selection.
- Final counts: train 19,966; validation 1,000; final test 1,000. Removed 34 training rows. Zero exact normalized overlap remains across splits. Near-duplicate/paraphrase overlap has not been checked.

Tokenizer and token counts:

- Special IDs: pad=0, bos=1, eos=2, unk=3. Byte-level alphabet covers all bytes; no unknown IDs occurred in the prepared stories or the additional Unicode checks.
- NFC normalization, no automatic prefix space, minimum merge frequency 2, tokenizer parallelism disabled; library versions and the saved tokenizer hash are in the manifest.
- Training text tokens: 4,381,513; plus one EOS per story: 4,401,479 saved token IDs.
- Validation text tokens: 215,320; including EOS: 216,320.
- Final test text tokens: 212,472; including EOS: 213,472.
- Saved streams contain text IDs followed by one EOS for each story; no automatic BOS, padding or fixed-length packing has been applied. Document offsets are preserved, allowing future packing/masking to be implemented explicitly.
